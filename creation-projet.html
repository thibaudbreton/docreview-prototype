Processing mode
How much of the initial work the AI does. Nothing here is irreversible: the AI never overwrites a manual correction, and you can re-run any step later.
AI-assisted Recommended
The AI segments the document, proposes a characterization and an assignment for each requirement. You review and validate — nothing is committed without you.
AI segmentation only
The AI splits the document into blocks and detects tables. Characterization and assignment are entirely yours — no AI suggestions anywhere.
Fully manual No AI
No AI processing at all. You select passages in the document to create requirements, then characterize and assign them yourself.
Individual steps Custom
Capture
Splits the document into text blocks, detects tables and flags uncertain boundaries.
Characterizer
Proposes a type and domain for each block, and whether it is a requirement.
Compliance matrix
Proposes an expert based on domain and past tenders.
Typology detection
Detects whether a requirement is SIG, Mainline, Turnkey or other.

Manual mode. The review screen opens with the document but no blocks. Use Edit segmentation to draw requirement boundaries, then characterize and assign them from the right panel.
